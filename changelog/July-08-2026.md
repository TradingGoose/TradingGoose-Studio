### Design Decisions
- Dashboard layouts are intentionally split across independent persistence owners: `layoutMaps.layout` stores topology, `layout_widgets` stores per-panel widget params and pair color, `layout_pairs` stores shared linked color-pair context, and referenced entities keep their own existing Yjs documents. `apps/tradinggoose/widgets/layout-document.ts` and `apps/tradinggoose/lib/dashboard-layouts/read-projection.ts` are the only places that assemble the read-time aggregate projection.
- Watchlists remain shared workspace root entities with `userId = null` and `parentId = null`, and sections are limited to one level under the root. `apps/tradinggoose/lib/watchlists/validation.ts` enforces canonical ids and hierarchy shape before `materializeWatchlistDocumentInTx()` persists changes.
- Saved-entity identities are DB-owned metadata, not editable Yjs content fields. Layout, watchlist, workflow, skill, custom tool, indicator, knowledge-base, and MCP rename paths all flow through `renameSavedEntityIdentity()` and refresh disposable entity-list projections after commit.
- Realtime mutation durability favors explicit replay and failure signaling over stale fallbacks. `beginRealtimeMutationTransaction()` records request fingerprints and serialized results, `runYjsDrainFencedTransaction()` fences revocation-sensitive mutations, and `requireWorkflowRealtimeState()` raises `WorkflowRealtimeRequiredError` when editable workflow state cannot be read from the live Yjs path.
- Personal dashboard availability is guaranteed by provisioning on workspace bootstrap and permission grants. The removed `apps/tradinggoose/lib/workspaces/entity-documents.ts` last-entity guard is not part of the current design; dashboard delete protection is now limited to owner locking plus the explicit `Cannot delete active layout` rule in `apps/tradinggoose/lib/dashboard-layouts/operations.ts`.
