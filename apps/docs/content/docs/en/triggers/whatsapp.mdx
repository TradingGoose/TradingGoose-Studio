## Setup Instructions

<div class="mb-3"><strong>1.</strong> Go to your <a href="https://developers.facebook.com/apps/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer" class="text-muted-foreground underline transition-colors hover:text-muted-foreground/80">Meta for Developers Apps</a> page and navigate to the "Build with us" --> "App Events" section.</div><div class="mb-3"><strong>2.</strong> If you don't have an app:<br /><ul class="mt-1 ml-5 list-disc"><li>Create an app from scratch</li><li>Give it a name and select your workspace</li></ul></div><div class="mb-3"><strong>3.</strong> Select your App, then navigate to WhatsApp > Configuration.</div><div class="mb-3"><strong>4.</strong> Find the Webhooks section and click "Edit".</div><div class="mb-3"><strong>5.</strong> Paste the <strong>Webhook URL</strong> above into the "Callback URL" field.</div><div class="mb-3"><strong>6.</strong> Paste the <strong>Verification Token</strong> into the "Verify token" field.</div><div class="mb-3"><strong>7.</strong> Click "Verify and save".</div><div class="mb-3"><strong>8.</strong> Click "Manage" next to Webhook fields and subscribe to <code>messages</code>.</div>
