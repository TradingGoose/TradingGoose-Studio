## Setup Instructions

<div class="mb-3"><strong>1.</strong> Go to <a href="https://app.fireflies.ai/settings" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">app.fireflies.ai/settings</a></div><div class="mb-3"><strong>2.</strong> Navigate to the <strong>Developer settings</strong> tab</div><div class="mb-3"><strong>3.</strong> In the <strong>Webhook</strong> section, paste the Webhook URL above</div><div class="mb-3"><strong>4.</strong> Enter a <strong>Secret</strong> (16-32 characters) and save it here as well</div><div class="mb-3"><strong>5.</strong> Click <strong>Save</strong> in Fireflies to activate the webhook</div><div class="mb-3"><strong>6.</strong> Your workflow will now trigger when any meeting transcription completes</div>
